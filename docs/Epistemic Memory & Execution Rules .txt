# Epistemic Memory & Execution Rules

## The one-way rule

Knowledge moves in exactly **one direction**: from raw reality to derived
understanding. Raw records are never rewritten by anything derived from them.
That is the whole rule; everything below is which layer does what.

The common mis-drawing puts it as *Chronicle → Hindsight → Logos*. That is
wrong in three ways: Hindsight is a **store, not a stage**; the raw store is
**Foundation**, not Chronicle; and **Logos is not the endpoint** — it reasons
and writes its proposals back into the system. The corrected version follows.

## The pipeline

**1. Foundation — raw reality.**
Foundation holds immutable observations: the unfiltered record of what was
actually captured (screen text, windows, events). Everything entering it is
stamped `observation`. It never holds a derived statement, and nothing derived
may overwrite it. This is the source of truth, not a processing step.

**2. Cognition — the derived lifecycle owner.**
Cognition owns everything *derived from* that raw reality: hypotheses,
patterns, open questions, relationships, and Kairos episodes. It is the
**lifecycle owner**: a derived statement moves through
`proposed → testing → corroborated → confirmed | rejected`. Cognition only
stores records and books those transitions. It **never reasons** — it does not
invent, weigh evidence, or judge.

**3. Hindsight — the derived store.**
Hindsight is where the derived records are **mirrored** for recall, tagged in
one canonical `gaia:*` namespace. It is a *store*, not a pipeline stage:
records are written into it from Cognition, and it is fully **rebuildable from
Cognition**. Its job is to persist and to make derived knowledge findable —
not to sit between raw input and reasoning.

**4. Logos — the reasoning faculty.**
Logos is where understanding is formed: it interprets intent, weighs evidence,
and **proposes** hypotheses and patterns. Crucially, Logos is **not an
endpoint that receives hypotheses**. It is a faculty that runs (reflection
happens *after* a reply is delivered) and writes its proposals **into
Cognition** — the lifecycle owner. Logos proposes; it owns no truth.

## Who confirms — the Absolute Override

Only a **human** moves a statement to `confirmed`. No model confidence, no
corroboration, and no automation ever reaches it. This is the Absolute
Override, and it lives in the human review surface (GaiaChat). Confirmation is
the one act that no part of the pipeline can perform on its own.

## The corrected shape, in words

Reality flows forward and never loops back:

    Foundation (raw, "observation")
          ↓  is interpreted, never overwritten
    Cognition (owns the derived lifecycle: hypotheses · patterns · episodes)
          ↓  is mirrored into
    Hindsight (derived store, gaia:*, rebuildable from Cognition)

    Logos runs alongside this, reasoning and forming proposals,
    and writes them back into Cognition.
    Only a human confirms.

## Corollary: the popular diagram, corrected

| The mis-drawing says | What is actually true |
| --- | --- |
| Chronicle → Hindsight → Logos | Foundation → Cognition (mirrored to Hindsight); Logos writes back into Cognition |
| "Chronicle (Observations)" is the raw store | Foundation is the raw store; Chronicle is the capture client that feeds it |
| Hindsight sits in the middle of the flow | Hindsight is a derived *store* on the side, not a stage between raw and reasoning |
| Logos is the final consumer of "hypotheses" | Logos *forms proposals*; Cognition owns them; only a human confirms |
| "Interpretations" live at Hindsight | Derivations live in Cognition; Hindsight mirrors them for recall |

The kernel of truth the original diagram was reaching for: there **is** a
strict epistemic reduction from raw to derived, and it runs one way only. The
correction is in the *entities*, not in that principle.
